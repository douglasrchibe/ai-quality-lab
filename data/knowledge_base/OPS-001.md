Production deployments are allowed Tuesday through Thursday between 09:00 and 15:00 local time.

Every production change requires two approvals: one from a peer engineer and one from the service owner.

A tested rollback plan is mandatory. If error rates rise after a release, the deploying engineer must roll back within 30 minutes.

A change freeze applies during the last two weeks of December. Only emergency fixes approved by the incident commander may be deployed during the freeze.

Database schema migrations must be backward compatible with the previous application version.
