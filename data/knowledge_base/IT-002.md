title: Remote Access and VPN Standard
category: it_policy
classification: internal
version: 2
effective_date: 2025-06-01
status: current
---
All access to internal systems from outside a Northwind office must go through the GlobalConnect VPN. Split tunneling is disabled for all users.

Only company-managed devices may connect to the VPN. Personal devices may use webmail and the HR portal through the browser, but never the VPN.

When working on public Wi-Fi, employees must connect to the VPN before opening any internal application. VPN sessions disconnect automatically after 12 hours.
