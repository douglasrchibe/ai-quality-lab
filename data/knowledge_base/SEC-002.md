Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery, by calling extension 7777 or emailing soc@northwind.example.

Incidents are classified as SEV1, SEV2, or SEV3. SEV1 means confirmed data exposure or a production-wide outage caused by an attack.

For a SEV1 incident, the on-call incident commander must page the Chief Information Security Officer within 15 minutes.

Do not power off a compromised machine. Disconnect it from the network and preserve it for forensic analysis.

A written post-incident review is due within 5 business days of closing a SEV1 or SEV2 incident.
