id: SEC-003
title: Browser Extension Security Standard
category: security_policy
classification: internal
version: 1
effective_date: 2026-03-01
status: current
---
Effective March 2026, every browser extension must pass a security review before installation on a company device. This applies to free and paid extensions.

Requests for a security review are submitted through the IT Service Desk portal. Approved extensions are listed in the extension catalog.

Extensions that request access to all websites are approved only for roles with a documented business need.
