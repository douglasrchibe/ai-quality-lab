Northwind Insight is the company's analytics platform for customer usage data.

Customer data is retained for 13 months, after which it is permanently deleted.

Reports can be exported as CSV or Parquet files. Scheduled exports run nightly at 02:00 UTC.

Northwind Insight supports single sign-on through SAML 2.0. SCIM provisioning is available on the Enterprise plan only.

The public API is limited to 600 requests per minute per workspace.
