This policy replaces version 1 of the password policy.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several unrelated words are recommended.

Passwords must be changed every 180 days, and immediately if a compromise is suspected. The last 10 passwords cannot be reused.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all accounts, including email, VPN, and cloud administration consoles. Hardware security keys are required for administrators.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes.
