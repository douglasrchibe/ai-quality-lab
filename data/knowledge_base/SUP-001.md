Support tickets are prioritized as P1, P2, or P3.

A P1 ticket means the product is unavailable for the customer. P1 tickets receive a first response within 1 hour, 24 hours a day.

P2 tickets receive a first response within 4 business hours. P3 tickets receive a first response within 2 business days.

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
