Northwind Systems provides laptops, accounts, and network access for business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work or create security risk.

Employees must lock their screen whenever they leave their device unattended. Company laptops must not be shared with family members or other non-employees.

Employees may install browser extensions without approval if the extension is free.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours. Violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.
